# demo_input.mp4 sources (all from the TEST subjects, never used for threshold selection)
Concatenation of 4 GMDCSA-24 clips (CC BY 4.0, Alam et al., Data in Brief 57 (2024) 110892, doi:10.5281/zenodo.13354453),
scaled to 1280x720 @ 30 fps, no other changes:
1. Subject 3/Fall/13.mp4 - writing while sitting on the floor, then falling backward (day)
2. Subject 3/Fall/16.mp4 - walking then falling left side on the floor (night)
3. Subject 4/ADL/16.mp4  - sitting on the bed, removing goggles, then sleeping (day) -> lying+still but NOT a fall: shows why a human decides
4. Subject 4/ADL/13.mp4  - writing while sitting on a chair (day)
